# Lab Technician Syllabus (AIIMS CRE Level)

## A. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Analogies, similarities and differences, space visualization, spatial orientation, problem solving, analysis, judgement, decision making, visual memory, discrimination, observation, relationship concepts, arithmetical reasoning and figural classification, arithmetic number series, non-verbal series, coding and decoding, statement conclusion, syllogistic reasoning.

## B. General Awareness
Everyday observations and experience in their scientific aspect. India and its neighbouring countries especially history, culture, geography, economic scene, general policy & scientific research.

## C. Quantitative Aptitude
Computation of whole numbers, decimals, fractions, percentages. Ratio & proportion, square roots, averages, interest, profit and loss, discount, partnership business, mixture and allegation, time and distance, time & work, basic algebraic identities, graphs of linear equations, basic geometry (triangles, circles, 3D solids), trigonometric ratio, standard identities, complementary angles, heights and distances, histogram, frequency polygon, bar diagram & pie chart.

## D. English Language
Spot the error, fill in the blanks, synonyms, antonyms, spellings, idioms & phrases, one word substitution, improvement of sentences, active/passive voice, direct/indirect narration, shuffling of sentence parts, cloze passage, comprehension.

## E. Subject Knowledge (Main Domain)

### Biochemistry
- Cleaning/care of glassware and equipment. Pipettes calibration.
- Distilled water preparation/storage.
- Preparation of solutions (weights, volume, concentration methods).
- SI units, CGS units, Normality, Molarity, Molality.
- Analytical balance maintenance. QC/QA in lab. Lab organization.
- Assay principles, normal ranges in Blood, Serum, Plasma, Urine.
- pH: Henderson-Hasselbalch, pKa, measurement (meters, papers).
- Volumetric analysis: standard/molar solutions.
- Electrophoresis (Paper, Agarose, PAGE).
- Chromatography (Paper, TLC, Ion Exchange, Affinity, Gas, HPLC).
- Centrifugation principles. Photometry, atomic absorption, spectrophotometry, colorimetry.
- Enzymes: basic concepts, mechanisms, factors influencing action.
- Carbohydrates: chemistry, metabolism, diagnostics (glucose, ketones, lactate, etc), Diabetes Mellitus.
- Lipids: chemistry, metabolism, lipoproteins, lipid disorder diagnostics.
- Proteins & amino acids: chemistry, metabolism, diagnostics.
- Nucleic acids basic concepts.
- Nutrition: Vitamins & Minerals (sources, biochemical roles, deficiency states, testing methods).
- Trace elements assessment.
- Electrolytes and acid-base balance/disorders.
- Endocrinology: RIA, ELISA, chemiluminescence for hormones (Thyroid, Adrenal).
- Molecular biology: DNA technology, PCR.
- Porphyrins/metabolism disorders.
- Renal, Hepatobiliary, Stomach, Pancreatic, Intestinal tract lab tests.
- Calcium and phosphate metabolism.

### Microbiology
- History, Host-Microbe relationship, safety measures, waste management.
- Microscopy, Sterilization, Disinfection.
- Bacterial morphology, growth/nutrition, culture media (aerobic/anaerobic).
- Handling lab animals, safety/QC. Antimicrobial susceptibility.
- Stains: Simple, Gram, Ziehl-Neelsen, Albert's, Capsule.
- Biochemical tests for bacterial identification.
- Immunity (innate, acquired, humoral, cell-mediated), Antigen-Antibody reactions, Complement, Hypersensitivity, Vaccines.
- Bacteriology (Coccis and Bacilli, Spirochaetes, Chlamydia, Mycoplasma, Rickettsia).
- Virology (DNA/RNA viruses, HIV, Dengue, SARS, Rabies, Hepatitis, etc.).
- Parasitology (Protozoa, Cestodes, Trematodes, Intestinal & Tissue Nematodes). Preservation of specimens.
- Mycology (General, culture, superficial/deep/opportunistic fungal infections).

### Pathology / Haematology / Blood Banking
- Haematology: origin/development of blood cells. Blood collection methods/anticoagulants. Automation.
- Hemocytometry (RBC/WBC counting).
- Haemoglobinometry, ESR. Reticulocyte count, Heinz bodies.
- Abnormal red cells / Anaemia (etiology, diagnosis, HPLC, sickling).
- Leucocyte reactions, leukemias, multiple myeloma.
- Coagulation: BT, CT, PT, APTT, platelet counts.
- LE cell, Blood parasites (Malaria, microfilaria, LD bodies).
- Blood Banking: Donor setup, collection, component prep. Blood grouping (ABO, Rh). Cross-matching (Coombs test etc), transfusion hazards.
- Histopathology: tissue processing, fixation, embedding, sectioning (microtomy), staining (H&E, special stains like PAS, Reticulin).
- Cytology: processing/staining (FNAC, PAP smear). Museum techniques.
